    Lake Pleasant Regional Park sits on the Bortle 5/6 urban-rural transition (19.50 to 18.95 mag/arcsec² at zenith, limiting naked-eye magnitude ~5.5). Stepping 1 mile north into the China Dam / Humbug Creek basin places low Bradshaw foothills between your tripod and Peoria/Phoenix lights, improving zenith contrast by ~0.3–0.5 mag after 15–20 minutes of dark adaptation.

    Route, Vehicle Clearance & Safety High-Clearance 4WD · Walk Final 1/4 Mi

    Access via Castle Hot Springs Rd north to Cow Creek Rd / Tip Top Mine Access Rd, dropping toward Humbug Creek. Trails have zero signage and numerous spider intersections—download an offline GPX track before losing signal. Park near the ridgeline or sediment-filled Upper China Dam and walk down to the breached 19th-century stone dam. Nearby Desert Tortoise Campground & Sunset Ridge (Lake Pleasant) offer paved alternatives.
